Japanese 100 deck data
The selection is based on the spoken Japanese high-frequency list documented by Cultivating Japanese, which cites A Frequency Dictionary of Japanese (Routledge, 2013) and a corpus from the National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics. Repeated grammatical entries were consolidated and readings and Russian glosses were prepared for this app.
Source: https://www.cultivatingjapanese.org/the-100-most-frequent-words-in-spoken-japanese/
